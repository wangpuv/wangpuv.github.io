概述

小步打卡是一款为儿童和家庭设计的习惯养成应用。我们非常重视您的隐私，尤其是儿童的数据安全。

简而言之：小步打卡不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。您的所有数据仅保存在您的设备上。

数据收集

小步打卡不会收集任何数据，包括但不限于：

  • 个人信息（姓名、邮箱、手机号等）
  • 位置信息
  • 设备标识符
  • 使用分析或崩溃报告
  • 照片、通讯录或其他设备上的数据

数据存储

应用内创建的所有数据（任务、打卡记录、积分、奖励）均仅存储在您的设备本地。我们不运营任何服务器或云服务。

第三方服务

小步打卡未集成任何第三方 SDK，包括：

  • 分析服务
  • 广告网络
  • 社交媒体 SDK
  • 崩溃报告工具

儿童隐私

小步打卡面向 6–15 岁儿童，在家长指导下使用。由于我们完全不收集任何数据，因此不存在儿童数据泄露的风险。

数据共享

我们不会与任何第三方共享任何数据，因为我们无法访问您的任何数据。

政策变更

如果我们更新本隐私政策，将在本页面发布修订版本并更新日期。

English

Overview

LittleSteps is a habit-building app designed for children and families. We take your privacy very seriously — especially when it comes to children's data.

In short: LittleSteps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

LittleSteps does not collect any data, including but not limited to:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Photos, contacts, or other on-device data

Data Storage

All data created within the app (tasks, check-in records, points, rewards) is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate any servers or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

LittleSteps does not integrate any third-party SDKs, including:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting tools

Children's Privacy

LittleSteps is designed for use by children aged 6–15 under parental guidance. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there is no children's data at risk.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with any third parties, because we do not have access to any of your data.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
