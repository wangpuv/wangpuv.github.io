Privacy Policy

LittleSteps is a habit-building app for children aged 6–15 and their families. Children should use it with parental guidance.

Tasks, categories, check-ins, points, rewards and other app settings are stored on the device. The parent PIN is stored locally, and the trial start date is stored in the device Keychain. The developer does not provide accounts, servers or cloud sync and does not receive this app content.

The app does not integrate advertising, usage analytics, social media or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not automatically collect names, contact details, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, photos or contacts for the developer.

Purchases and restoring purchases use Apple's StoreKit services. Apple handles the related account, payment and transaction information under its services and privacy policies. The app uses verified transaction results to determine access to the full app. Local storage of app content does not mean purchases work without Apple services.

Parents can choose to export or import backups and select a file location using the system interface. If you choose a cloud file provider or share a file with someone else, that file is handled under the chosen service and your actions. Keep backups safe and do not email children's data to support. A normal backup does not include the parent PIN, trial start date, purchase entitlement or reminder settings.

The developer cannot access this local app content and does not share it with third parties. Your device, Apple services and any file providers you choose operate under their own settings and policies.

Changes to this policy will be posted on the existing public page with an updated date. If you choose to email support, the information in your email is used to handle your request. Please send only the information needed.

Support

App language: Open the language entry from onboarding or from Management after parent verification, then choose the app language in iOS Settings. If no language option appears, check your system language settings. Return to the app or reopen it after the change. Saved tasks, rewards and notes keep their original text.

Trial and purchase: The app offers a 14-day trial. A parent can enter Management using their PIN and unlock the full app with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. The purchase screen shows the price provided by Apple. A purchase awaiting approval is not complete.

Restore purchases: Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. If the purchase is recognized automatically, the purchase entry is hidden and you can use the unlocked app. If you can still open the purchase screen, select Restore purchases. Cancelling sign-in or having no purchase to restore does not unlock the app. If an error appears, keep the message and try again later.

Backup and restore: After parent verification, open Backup and Restore in Management and export a JSON file for safekeeping. Back up your current data before importing. Selecting a file and confirming the import replaces the current business data. Normal backups do not include the parent PIN, trial start date, purchase entitlement or reminder settings. If an import is interrupted, keep the original file and resume with the same backup. Do not clear your data.

Contact support: Email bluefalconeye@163.com with the app version, device and OS version, app language, steps taken and error message. Do not send your PIN, Apple Account password or a child's original backup. Do not delete existing data to reproduce a problem.
